# Factorial Digit Sum (100!)

## Approach
- Compute `n!` using [integer](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/integer) [multiplication](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/multiplication) in a loop. Python's [integers](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/integer) have [arbitrary precision](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/arbitrary-precision-arithmetic), so `100!` is handled exactly.
- Convert the [factorial](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/factorial) value to a [string](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/string) and sum its digits.

## Correctness notes
- The statement provides `10! = 3628800` and [digit sum](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/digit-sum) `27`; we assert `factorial_digit_sum(10) == 27`.
- Then we compute and print the [digit sum](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/digit-sum) of `100!`.

## Complexity
- Time: `O(n * M)` where `M` is the cost of [big integer](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/arbitrary-precision-integer) [multiplication](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/multiplication) (very small for `n=100`).
- Space: `O(D)` where `D` is the number of [digits](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/numerical-digit) of `n!` (also small for `n=100`).
