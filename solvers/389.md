# Project Euler 389 Solution - Platonic Dice

<https://projecteuler.net/problem=389>:

* [389.py](389.py)

## Key idea: compound sums

Each stage is a **[random sum](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/random-sum) of [i.i.d.](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables) dice**:

- Roll a die to get a count `N`
- Roll `N` dice of the next type and sum them

If

- `Y` is one die roll ([mean](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/mean) `μ`, [variance](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/variance) `σ²`)
- `N` is the random number of dice ([mean](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/mean) `E[N]`, [variance](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/variance) `Var(N)`)
- `X = Y₁ + … + Y_N` with `Yᵢ` [i.i.d.](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/independent-and-identically-distributed-random-variables) and [independent](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/independence-of-random-variables) of `N`

then:

- `E[X] = E[N] · μ`
- `Var(X) = E[N] · σ² + Var(N) · μ²`

This is just the [law of total expectation](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/law-of-total-expectation) and the [law of total variance](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/law-of-total-variance):
`Var(X) = E[Var(X|N)] + Var(E[X|N])`.

Applying these two formulas repeatedly lets us propagate `(mean, variance)` through the whole chain without ever building the full [probability distribution](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/probability-distribution) (which would be enormous).

## Exact arithmetic

The [means](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/mean) and [variances](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/variance) of a fair `s`-sided die (`1..s`) are [rational numbers](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/rational-number):

- `μ = (s+1)/2`
- `σ² = (s²−1)/12`

The implementation uses `fractions.Fraction` to keep all intermediate results exact, and only rounds at the very end.

## Output formatting

[Rounding](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/rounding) to 4 decimal places is done with [integer arithmetic](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/integer-arithmetic) (scaled [numerator](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/numerator)/[denominator](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/denominator)) and **half-up** [rounding](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/rounding) to avoid any [floating-point arithmetic](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/floating-point-arithmetic) edge cases.

## Self-checks

A couple of lightweight assertions validate:

- the closed-form [mean](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/mean)/[variance](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/variance) for a standard die
- the compound-sum [variance](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/variance) formula against a tiny brute-force distribution (2-sided dice)
