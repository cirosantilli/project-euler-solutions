For a fixed parenthesization, the [coefficient](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/coefficient) of a leaf is `-1` to the power of
the number of right turns on its path from the root.  If `P_n(y)` is the sum,
over all Catalan parenthesizations with `n` minus signs, of these signed leaf
[polynomials](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/polynomial), then splitting at the root gives

`P(x, y) = 1 / (1 - x C(x) + x y C(x y))`,

where `C(x)` is the Catalan [generating function](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/generating-function).

The desired [sequence](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/sequence) is obtained by evaluating the leaf [polynomial](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/polynomial) on [Fibonacci
numbers](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/fibonacci-number):

`A(n) = (P_n(alpha) - P_n(beta)) / (alpha - beta)`,

where `alpha` and `beta` are the two roots of `t^2 - t - 1`.  This is an
[algebraic generating function](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/algebraic-generating-function), so its [coefficients](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/coefficient) satisfy a [polynomial-coefficient
recurrence relation](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/polynomial-coefficient-recurrence-relation).  The solver uses that [recurrence relation](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/recurrence-relation) [modulo](https://ourbigbook.com/codex/modulo-operation) `10^9 + 9`, with the given
small cases as assertions.
